                            Docs

                            What works where?

                            Camera, microphone, keyboard and speakers work in all current browsers. Battery info and speaker selection need Chrome, Edge or Opera. Camera and microphone need HTTPS or localhost and your permission.

                            How do stamps work?

                            A stamp is awarded when a test shows real results, such as every screen fill viewed or both speaker channels played. Speaker and display stamps record that the test ran; you judge by eye and ear whether it looked and sounded right.

                            Why does the drain tracker need the tab open?

                            A web page can only read the battery while it is running. Keep the tab open, even in the background, and plug-in periods are shown in green on the graph. Data stays in this browser for up to six hours.

                            What leaves my device?

                            Nothing, except when you press Start on the Network speed test, the Wi-Fi stability test or the website checks. Those talk to Cloudflare's speed servers and to the websites you choose. Every other check runs entirely in your browser.

                            Can I use it offline?

                            Yes. Open it once over HTTPS or localhost and it caches itself. In Chrome or Edge you can also use the install icon in the address bar to add it as an app.

                            Why can't I change settings?

                            Browsers can read and test hardware but can't change system settings such as keys, brightness or power plans. Use Windows settings for those.
