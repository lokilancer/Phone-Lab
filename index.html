                            Help

                            Which phones work?

                            Android phones with Chrome work best. Most checks also work in other Android browsers. iPhone support is planned for later, because Safari limits sensors, vibration and Bluetooth.

                            Why did a check ask for permission?

                            Camera, microphone, location and motion checks need your permission. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Location coordinates are never saved, and the report only keeps the accuracy.

                            What leaves my phone?

                            Nothing, except when you press Start on Network speed or Connection stability. They talk to Cloudflare's speed servers. Every other check runs entirely in the browser.

                            How do I install it?

                            Open it in Chrome, then use the menu and choose Add to home screen, or press the button on the Checks page when it appears. It then works offline.

                            How do stamps work?

                            A stamp is awarded when a check shows real results. An orange "!" means it found a problem, such as a weak GPS fix, a slow connection or no vibration felt.
